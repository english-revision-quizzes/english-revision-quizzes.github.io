record: noun or verb
He keeps a record of his marks. (noun = a written note)
She will <b>record</b> the lesson. (verb = save it)
survey: noun or verb
The students filled in a survey. (noun = questions)
We will <b>survey</b> the class. (verb = ask or examine)
drip and leak
A drip is one small drop of liquid.
A <b>leak</b> is a hole or crack that lets liquid or gas out.
in addition / instead of
In addition, we planted trees. (as well)
We cycled <b>instead of</b> driving. (in place of)
apologize for / to
apologize for the mistake (the reason)
apologize <b>to</b> the teacher (the person)
