تكتيك / أسلوب
caught in a trap
وقع في فخ = unable to escape from a dangerous or difficult situation. Example: I was caught in a trap and lost my money.
put on a friendly face
يرتدي وجها ودودا = to pretend to be kind or friendly, sometimes to hide bad intentions. Example: The scammer put on a friendly face, but I did not trust him.
take control of
يسيطر على / يتحكم في = to start controlling or managing someone or something. Example: I took control of the project after many problems appeared.
too good to be true
أفضل من أن يكون حقيقيا = so good that it seems unlikely to be real. Example: The offer was too good to be true, so Ahmed checked it carefully.
trust ... but verify
ثق ولكن تحقق = believe someone, but check the facts to be safe. Example: You can trust your friend, but verify the information before sharing it.
wolves in sheep's clothing
ذئاب في ثياب الحملان = people who seem kind or harmless but are actually bad or dangerous. Example: Some online scammers are wolves in sheep's clothing.
