Phrasal Verbs 🧩

Unit 1 · break / bring

🎓 Learn the phrasal verbs

Phrasal verbs with break and bring. Tap 🔊 to hear any word or sentence.

break down
break down = (a machine) stop working; (a person) lose control of feelings; (talks) fail
Our car broke down and we had to push it.
break in / into
break in / into = enter a place by force, to steal
The burglars broke in through the window.
break in
break in = interrupt a conversation
We were talking when she broke in.
break off
break off = break a piece from something; end a relationship or agreement
He broke off a piece of chocolate.
break out
break out = (war or fire) start suddenly
The First World War broke out in 1914.
break up
break up = stop school for a holiday; end a relationship
Jenny has just broken up with her boyfriend.
bring up
bring up = care for and raise a child
She was brought up by her grandmother.
bring out
bring out = produce a product, put it in the market
They have just brought out a new phone.
bring back
bring back = make someone recall memories
The photos brought back wonderful memories.
