What happens 📖

  • It is the third day of the fight. Santiago is weary, and his hands are swollen and bleeding, but his spirit is still sharp.
  • The fish is weaker now. It circles slowly deep below the boat, getting ready for its last struggle.
  • Santiago braces his feet and pulls in the line inch by inch. He says that pain is not an enemy. It tells him that he is alive.
  • At last the marlin rises. It is silver and purple in the water, longer than the boat, with a sword-like bill. Santiago thinks it is like a king of the sea.
  • He asks the fish to forgive him, because he must end the fight.
  • He tells the fish that they both fought well and gave everything they had.
  • The sea is calm, the sun is warm and a gentle wind carries the skiff toward the coast.
  • Santiago imagines how the villagers will react. He hopes people will see that he is still strong and capable of greatness.
  • But he knows that the smell of blood in the water will call the sharks, and he promises to fight them.
  • He feels proud and sad at the same time. In a half dream he sees the lions of his youth, and the boat sails on into the dark.
