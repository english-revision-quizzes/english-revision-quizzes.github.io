What happens 📖

  • Santiago is an old fisherman in a small Cuban village. The sea is his companion.
  • His skin is dark and leathery, and his hands are rough, cut and scarred. His eyes are still clear, bright and full of determination.
  • He has gone out into the Gulf Stream for 84 days and has caught nothing.
  • The younger fishermen laugh at him quietly and call him salao, the worst kind of unlucky.
  • Santiago still walks proudly, with his back straight.
  • Only one person stays loyal to him: the boy Manolin, who loves him like a grandfather.
  • Manolin's parents made him join a luckier boat, but he still helps Santiago every evening.
  • Santiago lives in a bare little hut with almost nothing, but he never complains.
  • The old man and the boy share a simple meal and talk about baseball.
  • Santiago plans to go far out on the 85th day to find a big fish, and he dreams of the lions he once saw in Africa.
