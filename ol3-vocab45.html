مدمر / خرب

rumor
rumor n
شائعة
There is a rumor about the new manager.
rumor v
يشيع
People rumor many things online.
find + object + adjective
I find the exam difficult.
Not: I find to do homework difficult.
help + (object) + infinitive / to + infinitive
My teacher helped me understand / to understand the lesson.
Both forms are correct.
She agreed to help (to) do the housework.
Without an object: help do / help to do.
I promised to help mum with the cooking.
help + object + with + noun.
make + object + adjective / infinitive
Her words made me angry.
make + object + adjective
The teacher made the students write quietly.
make + object + infinitive (no to)
The students were made to leave the class.
Passive: be made + to + infinitive
-ed and -ing adjectives
I am interested in science.
-ed describes how a person feels.
Science is interesting.
-ing describes the thing that causes the feeling.
The little cat looks frightened. The large dog is frightening.
Same rule.
career - profession - work - job
career: the long path of your working life
Dr Samira has a long career in teaching.
profession: a skilled job that needs training
Teaching is a noble profession.
work: effort or the place you work (uncountable)
I go to work every day.
job: a particular paid position
He got a new job as a driver.
