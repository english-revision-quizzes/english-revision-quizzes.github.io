What happens 📖

  • Santiago wakes before sunrise, when the sky is still dark and full of stars. He loves the quiet of this hour.
  • Manolin is already waiting with black beans, rice and a little coffee. Santiago thanks him and says he will need his strength today.
  • The boy helps carry the lines, gaff, harpoon and the sail. The sail is patched so many times that it looks like the flag of defeat, but Santiago carries it with pride.
  • On the way to the harbour, some fishermen greet him with respect and others look at him with pity. Santiago ignores both.
  • At the old but seaworthy skiff, Manolin asks to come along. Santiago says no, because the boy is now on a lucky boat.
  • Santiago says that 85 is his number. Today he will go farther out than the other fishermen dare to go.
  • At dawn he pushes the skiff away from the shore. Manolin wishes him good luck.
  • Alone on the water, he remembers his youth, when he saw lions playing on the golden beaches of Africa.
  • Far from land, he baits his lines with fresh sardines and waits patiently for the big fish.
