🎨 Colours and feelings

  • Red makes you feel active and excited.
  • Orange makes you feel talkative and adventurous.
  • Yellow makes you feel cheerful and impatient.
  • Blue makes you feel calm, confident and peaceful.
  • Green makes you feel relaxed and refreshed.
  • White makes you feel isolated and withdrawn.

⚠️ Important note

  • accustomed to = get used to (followed by -ing or a noun).
  • busy with (noun) = busy + (doing something).
  • agree on (something) / agree to (do something).
  • accuse of = charge with.
