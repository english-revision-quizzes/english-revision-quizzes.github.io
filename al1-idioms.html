Unit 1 · Idioms and fixed phrases

🎓 Learn the idioms

Idioms and fixed phrases from Unit 1. Tap 🔊 to hear any word or sentence.

once in a blue moon
once in a blue moon = rarely
I only see my sister once in a blue moon.
black and blue
black and blue = bruised
Jack fell down the stairs and is black and blue.
green fingers
green fingers = good at gardening
You definitely have green fingers.
is a bit green
is a bit green = not experienced
I was very green when I started working there.
a little white lie
a little white lie = a fib
We told her a white lie not to hurt her feelings.
in black and white
in black and white = very clear
Now the facts are known and everything is in black and white.
out and about
out and about = active and busy
She should be out and about in a few days.
clear the air
clear the air = resolve a problem
Sue cleared the air between us.
vanish into thin air
vanish into thin air = disappear completely
Where are my keys? Have they vanished into thin air?
won't take no for an answer
won't take no for an answer = determined / insistent
He won't take no for an answer.
refuse to play ball
refuse to play ball = not go along with
The company refused to play ball.
drive a hard bargain
drive a hard bargain = make a deal without compromising
He drove a hard bargain for the house.
