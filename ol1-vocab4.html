3. Collocations

  • do revision · raise animals · record knowledge / history · reflect values / traditions / beliefs
  • honor the past · make progress · stay united · respect and admiration · embrace progress

4. Expressions

  • close to · different from / to · happen step by step (slowly, little by little)
  • run deep (be very strong and old) · shape who we are · struggle hard to survive · with a single click (very easily)

5. Verb + preposition

allow ... to · depend on · live close to · pass down · pick up · move forward

6. Word families

  • achieve (v) → achievement (n) → achiever (person) → achievable (adj)
  • civilize (v) → civilization (n) → civilized (adj) · civil (adj: polite)
  • magnificent (adj) → magnificence (n) → magnificently (adv)
  • might (n: power) → mighty (adj) → mightily (adv)

7. Clear the confusion

  • advise (verb) / advice (noun, uncountable: a piece of advice)
  • challenge = something difficult but exciting · problem = something that causes trouble
  • evidence (uncountable, proof) · a clue (a hint that helps you find the answer)
  • figure = a shape, a person you cannot see clearly, or a number in statistics · number = a count
  • scene = part of a film or a place where something happens · scenery = beautiful natural view
  • valuable = worth a lot · available = free to use or meet
  • British -our / American -or: colour / color, favour / favor, flavour / flavor, honour / honor, labour / labor, neighbour / neighbor
