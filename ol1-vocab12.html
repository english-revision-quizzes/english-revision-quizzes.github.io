able to - capable of
(be) able to + infinitive

agree on / about - agree to - agree with

agree to + infinitive

agree with + someone
I agree with you.
along - across - through
along (prep / adv): next to, in the direction of
We walked along the river.
across / through (prep / adv): from one side to the other
She ran across / through the street.
be proud to - be proud of - take pride in
be proud to + infinitive
I am proud to represent my school.
be proud of + noun / verb-ing
She is proud of her son.
take pride in + noun / verb-ing
He takes pride in his work.
go shopping - do the shopping
go shopping: the activity of visiting shops
We went shopping for clothes.
do the shopping: buy what you need (food, house things)
I will do the shopping this afternoon.
mission - job - work
mission (n): an important task or aim
The rescue mission was dangerous.
job (n, countable): a task or piece of work
I have several jobs to do.
job (n, countable): paid work, a position
My job is teaching English.
work (n, uncountable): effort or things to do
I have too much work to do.
work (n, countable): a creation or product
Egypt has great works of engineering.
work (n, uncountable): the place where you work
I go to work by bus.
remember - remind
remember (v): keep something in your mind
I always remember my wife's birthday.
remind (v): make someone remember
Please, remind me to call my mother.
