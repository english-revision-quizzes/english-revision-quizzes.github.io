What happens 📖

  • The sun rises as the skiff sails toward the shore. Santiago feels uneasy, because he knows the sea never gives without asking for something in return.
  • Then he sees a dark shadow moving fast beneath the water. It is a shark.
  • It is a mako shark, fast and strong. It follows the trail of blood and attacks the marlin's side.
  • Santiago drives his harpoon deep into its head, and the shark sinks. But the marlin has lost a big piece of flesh.
  • Soon more sharks come. Two galanos, shovel-nosed sharks, bite at the marlin. Santiago's harpoon breaks off in one shark.
  • He ties his knife to an oar and uses it as a spear. He kills one shark and drives the other away.
  • The fight goes on for hours. By the afternoon half of the marlin is gone, and only the backbone and some flesh are left.
  • At night he fights with the oar, then the tiller and even broken pieces of wood. He has nothing left but his will.
  • At last the sharks stop coming. Santiago says that they beat him, but only the sharks beat the fish, not him.
  • He thinks of the boy. He has lost his catch, but not his dignity, and the skiff carries the skeleton of the marlin toward the shore.
