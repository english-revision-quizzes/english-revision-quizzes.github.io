fluency / proficiency
fluency = speaking smoothly and easily
Ahmed has fluency in English and can have conversations easily.
proficiency = a high level of skill (tested or earned)
Sara achieved proficiency in French after years of study.
international / foreign
international = between or involving many countries
The school offers international programs.
foreign = from another country
She is studying a foreign language.
participate / join
participate (in) = take part in an activity
Students are encouraged to participate in class discussions.
join = become a member of a group or team
I want to join the basketball team.
perspective / opinion
perspective = a way of seeing or thinking about something
Travel gives people a new perspective on life.
opinion = what you think or believe about something
In my opinion, the movie was excellent.
tolerance / respect
tolerance = accepting people who are different
Tolerance helps people live peacefully together.
respect = a good feeling that someone is important
We should show respect to our teachers.
