Chapter 3 · The Fish Is Hooked 🎣

What happens 📖

  • The sun is high. Santiago watches a bird circle above the water, because he knows it is looking for fish near the surface.
  • The bird dives and small tuna jump out of the water, frightened by something bigger below.
  • Santiago feels the line move slowly and steadily. He does not pull. He lets the big fish take the bait.
  • Then the line tightens hard and cuts into his hand. The fish is hooked.
  • The fish does not jump. It swims steadily out to sea and pulls the skiff after it.
  • Santiago eats a piece of tuna to keep his strength and drinks a little water.
  • He talks to the fish softly. He calls it his brother, but he promises to fight with all his heart to catch it.
  • At night the line burns his hands. He ties it across his shoulders to share the strain, but he never lets go.
  • He thinks of a great baseball champion who played with pain, and he tells himself that endurance is what matters.
  • At dawn the fish is still strong and has not tired. Neither has Santiago.
