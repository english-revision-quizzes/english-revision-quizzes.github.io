adopt / adapt
adopt = start using something new (ideas, policies, habits)
The company decided to adopt a new recycling policy.
adapt (to) = change to fit a new situation
Animals must adapt to survive in the desert.
adapt = change something to make it suitable
The chair was adapted for children.
adapt (into) = change a book into a film or play
The novel was adapted into a movie.
cut down / cut down on
cut down = make a tree fall by cutting it
They cut down the old tree.
cut down = reduce the number of something
The city cut down streetlights to save money.
cut down on = use or do less of something
We need to cut down on plastic bags.
go green
go green (phrase) = start protecting the environment
Our school is going green by recycling more.
go green (literal) = become green in colour
The area goes green in spring.
join / join in
join = become a member of a group
Mohammed will join the science club this year.
join = connect two things
Join the two wires together.
join in = take part in an activity
Please join in the class discussion.
misuse / overuse
misuse = use in a wrong way
Misusing medicines can be dangerous.
overuse = use too much
Overusing water leads to water shortage.
reusable / renewable
reusable = can be used again (a bag, a bottle)
I bought a reusable water bottle.
renewable = replaced naturally (energy, resources)
Solar energy is a renewable resource.
