abroad / aboard
abroad = in or to another country
She studied abroad last year.
aboard = on a ship, plane, bus or train
All passengers are aboard the bus.
anxious / nervous / worried / afraid
anxious = worried about the future
She felt anxious before her exam.
nervous = tense before something important
He was nervous about his interview.
worried = thinking about a problem
I am worried about my mother's health.
afraid = frightened of something
The child is afraid of the dark.
rich / enriching / enriched
rich = having a lot of something
The country is rich in resources.
enriching = giving you something valuable
Travel is an enriching experience.
enriched = made better by adding something
The flour is enriched with vitamins.
speak / say / tell
speak = use your voice; use a language
He can speak three languages.
say = express words
She said hello to me.
tell = give information to someone
Please tell me the truth.
