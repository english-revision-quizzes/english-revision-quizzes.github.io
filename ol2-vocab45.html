  • oblige (v) → obliged (adj) → obligation (n) → obligatory (adj)
  • protect (v) → protection (n) → protective (adj) → protectively (adv)
  • rescue (v, n) → rescuer (n) → rescuable (adj) · risk (n, v) → risky (adj)
  • trust (v, n) → trusted (adj) → mistrust (v, n)
  • argue for = give reasons to support something · argue against = give reasons not to do it
  • put out a fire (stop it burning) · turn off a light or a machine
  • each other = two people · one another = more than two
  • Spelling: British -ise (organise, realise) = American -ize (organize, realize)
  • Sentence adverbs: certainly, honestly, in fact, luckily, unfortunately (they show the speaker's opinion of the whole sentence)
