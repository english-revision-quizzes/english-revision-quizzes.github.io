Unit 2 · Past tenses

🎓 Learn the grammar

Past tenses and used to / would from Unit 2. Tap 🔊 to hear any word or sentence.

Form
past form of regular or irregular verbs
go → went, visit → visited
Uses
an action at a definite time in the past
She visited her aunt last Friday.
past habits and states that were true in the past
I read adventure stories when I was young.
Form
was / were + verb + ing
He was watching TV all evening.
Uses
an action in progress at a certain time in the past
At 8 yesterday, I was doing my homework.
two actions in progress at the same time (while)
Mother was preparing dinner while we were playing.
a background action when another action took place (when)
He was watching TV when the lights went out.
Form
had + past participle
She had left.
Use
an action that took place before a certain time or another past action
When we got there, she had left.
Patterns
When the police arrived, the thief had run away. (the thief ran first)
When I arrived, she had left.
As soon as she had finished the e-mail, she sent it.
As soon as I had arrived, I phoned you.
Because I hadn't studied hard enough, I failed.
I failed because I hadn't studied.
Until: I hadn't been abroad until I went to London.
She hadn't passed until she studied harder.
After + past perfect; before + past simple
We got to the station after the train had left.
By + a time: By 9 o'clock we had finished the project.
By the time I got there, they had eaten.
already, just, yet with the past perfect
When Dad came home, we hadn't finished yet.
Form
had been + verb-ing
She had been crying all night.
Uses
ongoing actions that began before another action in the past
Her eyes were red; she had been crying.
the duration of an action before another past action
I had been walking for half an hour when I realised I was lost.
Used to + infinitive
past habits and states that are no longer true
I used to have a red bike when I was young.
Affirmative: subject + used to + infinitive
I used to like bananas.
Negative: subject + didn't use to + infinitive
My parents didn't use to have free time.
Question: Did + subject + use to + infinitive?
Did you use to work hard?
Would + infinitive
past repeated actions only (not states)
My mother would cook chicken every Sunday.
Negative: would not (wouldn't) + infinitive
My parents wouldn't leave me alone.
Question: Would + subject + infinitive?
Would your grandmother buy water in the past?
To sum up
Past simple: when an action happened
She visited her aunt last Friday.
Past continuous: an action was taking a period of time
He was watching TV all evening.
Past perfect: which of two actions happened first
When we got there, she had left.
Past perfect continuous: how long an action took before another past action
She had been learning English for two years before she took the job.
Used to / would: states, habits and repeated actions in the past
I used to smoke.
We would walk to school.
