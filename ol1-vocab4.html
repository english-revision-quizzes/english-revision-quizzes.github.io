advise - advice
advise (v): to tell someone what to do
The teacher advised me to study harder.
advice (n, uncountable): useful suggestions
I need your advice about my career.
a piece of advice / pieces of advice
Let me give you one piece of advice.
challenge - problem
challenge (n): something difficult that tests you
Climbing the mountain was a big challenge.
problem (n): something that causes trouble
Traffic is a big problem in the city.
evidence - clue
evidence (n, uncountable): facts that prove something
The police found evidence of the crime.
clue (n): a sign that helps you find the answer
The detective found a clue in the letter.
figure - number
figure (n): a statistic, or a shape you see
The sales figures have increased this year.
figure (n): a person or shape you cannot see clearly
I saw a strange figure behind the glass window.
number (n): a count, or a phone number
Write your phone number here.
scene - scenery
scene (n): part of a film, or where something happens
The last scene of the movie was sad.
scene (n): the place of an event
The police examined the crime scene.
scenery (n): beautiful natural view
The mountain scenery is wonderful.
valuable - available
valuable (adj): worth a lot of money or very useful
This necklace is very valuable.
available (adj): free to use, buy or meet
The manager is not available now.
British -our / American -or
colour / color · favour / favor
British English uses -our, American English uses -or.
flavour / flavor · honour / honor
We honor our past.
labour / labor · neighbour / neighbor
My neighbor keeps the street clean.
