Phrasal Verbs 🧩

Unit 2 · come / carry

🎓 Learn the phrasal verbs

Phrasal verbs with come and carry. Tap 🔊 to hear any word or sentence.

be carried away
be carried away = become very excited
We were carried away when Amr Diab appeared.
carry ... off
carry ... off = handle a difficult situation successfully
She was nervous, but she carried it off very well.
carry on
carry on = continue
You just have to carry on as if nothing happened.
carry out
carry out = do or complete a task or mission
Our soldiers carried out a successful attack.
come across
come across = find or meet by chance
He came across some old photos in the attic.
come down with
come down with = become ill
I think I'm coming down with flu.
come round
come round = visit casually; recover consciousness
Why don't you come round tonight?
come out
come out = appear; be published; become known; be removed (stains); blossom
When does their new album come out?
come up with
come up with = suggest or think of an answer
She came up with an amazing plan.
come into
come into = inherit money
She came into a lot of money when her grandfather died.
