Unit 1 · Egypt's Heritage

Long ago, people lived in ways very different from today. They depended on the land, growing crops and raising animals for survival. Families lived close to nature, often using simple tools and working together to produce what they needed. Communities were small, and traditions were passed down through stories, songs, and daily practices.

As time moved forward, inventions began to change human life. The discovery of writing allowed people to record knowledge and history. The use of metal tools made farming and building easier. Great civilizations rose, building cities, temples, and trade routes that connected distant lands.

While our ancestors were struggling hard to survive, their minds never stopped thinking till progress happened step by step.

Now, life has become much faster and more complex. Technology allows us to communicate across the world in seconds, travel long distances in hours, and access information with a single click. Yet, the wisdom and lessons from long ago still shape who we are. By remembering the past and learning from it, we can build a future that respects history while embracing progress.

💡 Reading tips

  • Read once for the main idea, then again for the details.
  • The bold words are key vocabulary from Lessons 4 & 5. Tap 🔊 to hear each paragraph.
  • Notice how the tenses change: past simple for finished actions (people lived), past continuous for an action in progress (were struggling), and present perfect for the change up to now (life has become).

One day we say, "I wouldn't be seen dead in it," and the next day we're wearing it! The big fashion houses in London, Paris and Milan decide what we should wear from year to year. For a time, it is fashionable for men to wear narrow trousers and wide ties until baggy trousers and narrow ties take their place.

Fashion is a term used to describe the creation of clothing, footwear, accessories, cosmetics and jewellery. Fashion influences not only the clothes we wear but also our shoes, hair, handbags and jewellery. Even if we think we can ignore fashions, we have to buy the clothes that designers think we should wear.

Following fashion is important to the extent that it is part of who you are and where you are going. You can follow fashion, relate it to your personal life but not necessarily "be in it" or have a closet full of innovative designs. For example, you might follow trends and trendsetters, know what designers are doing for the next season but stick to your regular jeans and T-shirt attire but have that special bag that keeps your look sharp and smart. I would consider that fashion.

💡 Words to know

  • I wouldn't be seen dead in it = I hate it so much that I would never wear it.
  • fashionable = popular and stylish at a certain time.
  • take their place = replace them.
  • Fashion house = a company that designs and sells fashionable clothes.
1 · Start
Say when it happened: Last Friday, One evening, Two years ago.
Say what your story is about in one sentence.
2 · Middle
Tell the events in order using past simple.
Use past continuous for the background action: I was walking...
Use when / while / as to join them.
3 · End
Say how the story finished.
Add a feeling: It was a day I will never forget.
Useful linkers
first, then, after that, suddenly, at that moment
while / as + past continuous
when + past simple
in the end, finally

Model paragraph: A strange day in my life

Last Friday was the strangest day of my life. I was walking to school when I noticed a small golden key on the pavement. While I was picking it up, a cat suddenly ran past me and stopped at an old wooden door. I was curious, so I tried the key, and the door opened slowly. Inside, there was a tiny library full of dusty books about Egypt's heritage. I was reading one of them when the school bell rang in the distance. I ran out and arrived at school just in time. In the end, I never found that door again, and it is still a mystery.

✅ Why this paragraph works

  • was walking (long action) + noticed (short action): when joins them.
  • While + past continuous: While I was picking it up...
  • Every sentence is in the past. Do not jump to the present.
  • Write about 8 sentences, as in the book exercises.

Model: A visit to the Grand Egyptian Museum

Last month, my class visited the Grand Egyptian Museum near the pyramids of Giza. We arrived at nine o'clock, and the guide welcomed us at the entrance. First, we walked up the huge staircase while the guide was telling us about the ancient kings. Then we saw giant statues and beautiful objects that were thousands of years old. I felt proud because this heritage belongs to my country. After that, we took photos and bought small souvenirs. We were tired when we left, but we were very happy. It was a trip I will never forget.

Model: Our country Egypt

Egypt is a country in North Africa, and the great River Nile runs through it. It has a civilization that is thousands of years old. Ancient Egyptians built the pyramids and temples, and they invented papyrus to record their history. Millions of tourists visit our monuments every year. The people of Egypt are friendly, and they take pride in their heritage. Today, we must preserve our monuments for generations to come. I am proud to be Egyptian.

📝 Writing checklist

  • Plan first: opening, 3-4 detail sentences, closing.
  • Use words from the unit: heritage, monument, civilization, preserve, pride, ancestors.
  • Check tenses, spelling and capital letters before you finish.
