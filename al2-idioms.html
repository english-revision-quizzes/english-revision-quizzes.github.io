Unit 2 · Idioms and fixed phrases

🎓 Learn the idioms

Idioms and fixed phrases from Unit 2. Tap 🔊 to hear any word or sentence.

as silent as the grave
as silent as the grave = very quiet
It's as silent as the grave in here.
as sick as a dog
as sick as a dog = sick and vomiting a lot
I was as sick as a dog after last night's meal.
as sick as a parrot
as sick as a parrot = very disappointed
He was as sick as a parrot when his team lost.
as tough as old boots
as tough as old boots = strong (person); difficult to eat (food)
The meat is as tough as old boots.
as different as chalk and cheese
as different as chalk and cheese = completely different
They are twins but they are like chalk and cheese.
as good as gold
as good as gold = behaving very well (a child)
The kids have been as good as gold.
as keen as mustard
as keen as mustard = very eager
Jenny is as keen as mustard.
be on the cards
be on the cards = likely to happen
I think there's a promotion for you. It is on the cards.
for a good cause
for a good cause = to help other people
Chris always does work for a good cause.
make it clear
make it clear = explain easily
I made it clear that I am not in love with her.
have your head in the clouds
have your head in the clouds = be unaware of the facts
She always has her head in the clouds.
get hot under the collar
get hot under the collar = become angry
He got rather hot under the collar.
