3. Collocations

  • fill her with optimism · mirror her mood · make a difference · your heart grows bigger · confidence grows
  • feel / be isolated from · on Thursday afternoon · for the first time in days

4. Expressions

  • wrap around ... like a gentle hug = surround someone in a warm, comforting way
  • light up = suddenly look happy · speak up = say your opinion bravely

5. Verb + preposition

fill ... with · speak up (about something) · feel isolated from · be resistant to

6. Word families

  • confidence (n) → confident (adj) → confidently (adv)
  • fade (v) → faded (adj) · fading (adj)
  • resist (v) → resistance (n) → resistant (adj) → resistible / irresistible (adj)
  • isolate (v) → isolated (adj) → isolation (n) → isolating (adj)
  • optimist (person) · optimism (n) · optimistic (adj)
  • overwhelm (v) → overwhelmed (adj) · transform → transformation (n) → transformative (adj)
  • volunteer (n, v) · volunteering (n) · voluntary (adj) · mood → moody (adj) · slip → slippery (adj)

7. Clear the confusion

  • stare = look for a long time · glance = look quickly
  • mood = how you feel now · moody = changing moods quickly and easily
  • voluntary (adj) = done by choice · volunteer (n) = the person who does it
