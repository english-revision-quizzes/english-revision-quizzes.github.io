Learning a foreign language opens the door to new cultures, ideas, and opportunities. If students learn a new language, they will be able to communicate with people from different countries, make international friends, and participate in global activities.

Moreover, they will understand other cultures better, which promotes respect, tolerance, and a broader worldview. If students practiced speaking regularly, they would improve their confidence, fluency, and ability to express themselves clearly in different situations. Schools that provide language programs encourage students to use these skills in real-life experiences, such as traveling, participating in exchange programs, joining international competitions, or using digital communication tools to connect with peers worldwide.

Some students hesitate to learn another language because they feel it is too difficult or time-consuming. However, if they tried consistently and remained motivated, they would gain not only language skills, but also critical thinking, problem-solving abilities, memory improvement, and new perspectives on the world.

In addition, if students spoke multiple languages, they would have more opportunities for higher education and careers in international companies. In short, learning a foreign language is not just about grammar and vocabulary. It is a key to becoming a global citizen, expanding personal horizons, and contributing positively to a connected and multicultural world.

My name is Salma, and I speak Arabic, English, and Spanish. I grew up in Cairo, but when I was twelve, my family moved to Spain because of my father's job. At first, I couldn't understand anything at school. I felt shy, anxious, and overwhelmed. However, my teachers encouraged me to practice every day with my classmates, and slowly I improved.

After a year, I could speak Spanish fluently and participate in class confidently. Later, I returned to Egypt and studied English more seriously. Speaking three languages has completely changed my life. I am now multilingual, which allows me to work with international companies, travel easily, and communicate with people from different cultures.

I believe learning languages is not just about words. It's about understanding people. We must respect the cultures connected to the languages we learn. In the future, I hope to study French. Who knows? Maybe one day I will speak five languages!

Learning any foreign language has become easier thanks to modern technology. In the past, students relied only on textbooks and classroom lessons. Today, digital tools provide many opportunities to practice and improve skills in fun and interactive ways.

One of the most popular methods is using language learning applications. These apps offer short daily lessons, vocabulary games, and much pronunciation practice. Many of them use artificial intelligence to adjust the level of difficulty according to the learner's progress. Some learners spend a little time every day, but they still make great progress.

Another useful tool is online video and audio platforms. By watching movies, listening to songs, or following podcasts in the target language, learners can hear natural pronunciation and improve listening skills. Subtitles and playback options make it simple to understand and repeat. However, only a few students make full use of these options.

Social media and messaging platforms also play a role. Learners can connect with native speakers around the world, exchange messages, and even join live video calls. This creates real communication opportunities without traveling abroad. But some students show little interest in using these chances effectively.

Finally, translation software and online dictionaries help students check meanings quickly and build confidence. When used together, these tools turn technology into a powerful partner for mastering new languages. Even if students have few resources, modern tools can still give them much support to reach success.

Ramy had always dreamed of traveling abroad. One summer, he finally flew to Liverpool, England. At first, he was excited, but soon he felt anxious. His biggest difficulty was that he couldn't speak English. At the airport, he struggled to understand the officer's questions. He only smiled and showed his passport, hoping for the best.

In the city, life became more complicated. When he tried to buy a bus ticket, he couldn't explain himself. People behind him grew impatient, and he felt embarrassed. Later, at a restaurant, he wanted chicken but accidentally received fish.

That night, Ramy decided something important: he must learn English. He downloaded a language app, practiced basic phrases, and wrote down useful words. The next day, he tried saying, "One ticket, please," and "Chicken, please." To his surprise, people understood him! He felt more confident.

As days passed, Ramy spoke more words, greeted locals, and even asked for directions. People appreciated his efforts and were kind to him. By the end of his trip, Ramy realized that learning English had turned his journey from a struggle into an enriching adventure.

Reading passage: Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a new technology which aims at making machines think and learn like humans. AI is now used in many applications. It is used in smartphones, making cars, flying planes and navigating ships. AI can help us to perform tasks quickly and accurately.

In the field of health care, robots help doctors to perform operations, make accurate diagnoses and decide the best treatment for patients. Thus, they can save lives and improve people's health.

In the field of industry, robots are used to carry out difficult tasks which people cannot do. They improve the quality of goods, reduce costs and increase production.

Moreover, AI is used in banks to do difficult calculations in no time. Even at homes, AI is used in modern home devices. Now, you can give voice orders to electric appliances to turn on and off.

In some modern countries, it has become normal to see driverless cars in streets. Just sit in the car and read a book while the car drives itself using GPS and some sensors to reach its destination.

In short, Artificial Intelligence offers great help to humanity. It has become a great factor to improve our life and boost our lives.

What is an informal email?
A personal message to friends, family or people you know well, in a relaxed, friendly style.
Why do we write one?
To share news, ask about someone, invite, thank, apologize or chat.
When do we use it?
For friends, classmates, relatives and close people. Not for business or official messages.
Style and language
Simple sentences, contractions (I'm, don't), everyday words, sometimes emojis, a friendly tone.
Structure
1 Greeting → 2 Opening → 3 Main message (clear paragraphs) → 4 Closing → 5 Your name
Typical openings and closings
Hi (name), · Hello, · What's up? · How's it going? · Long time no see!
Take care, · Best wishes, · See you soon! · Talk to you soon! · Love, · Yours,
Useful words
informal email
a personal message to friends, family or people you know well
greeting
a friendly start such as Hi or Hello
opening
a friendly first line such as How are you?
main message
the topic, news, invitation or information
closing
a friendly ending such as See you soon!
contraction
a short form such as I'm, don't, can't
catch up
to meet or talk and share recent news
let me know
tell me / inform me

Model email: inviting a friend on a weekend trip

Subject: Let's go on a weekend adventure!

Hi Nour,

How's everything going? I haven't seen you in a while, so I thought it would be great to catch up and have some fun together. I'm planning a weekend trip with a few friends, and I really hope you can join us!

We're heading to the beach this Saturday morning. We'll swim, play some volleyball, and have a picnic by the sea. I know how much you love the water and good food! If you want, you can bring your brother or anyone else who wants to come.

We'll meet at the park at 8:30 a.m., then we'll all go together by car. I'll bring snacks and drinks, but if you have a favorite, let me know!

Let me know as soon as you can if you're in. I can't wait to spend the day together and make some awesome memories!

See you soon,

Reda

⚠️ Important note

  • Use a friendly greeting and your friend's name.
  • Contractions are fine in an informal email.
  • Organize your ideas in clear paragraphs.
  • End with a friendly closing, then your name.
