3. Expressions and collocations

  • preserve heritage · leave a legacy · play a role (in)
  • take pride (in) · stand with pride · lack of awareness
  • meet in harmony (live together peacefully) · carry its past with it (still shows its history)
  • for thousands of years · for generations to come · a crossroads for trade
  • "Egypt's heart has beaten for thousands of years" = its culture has continued for a very long time.

4. Verb + preposition

carve ... into · contribute to + noun / -ing · encourage somebody to + verb · reconnect with · share ... with · welcome ... back (to)

5. Word families

  • preserve → preservation (n), preserved (adj), preservative
  • pride (n) → proud (adj) → proudly (adv)
  • restore → restoration (n), restored (adj)
  • heritage (n) → inherit (v) → inheritance (n)
  • identity (n) → identify (v) → identification (n)
  • merchant (person) / merchandise (goods) · monument / monumental (adj) · neighbor / neighborhood / neighboring

6. Clear the confusion

  • able to + verb, capable of + noun / -ing: She is able to solve it. He is capable of leading.
  • agree on / about + noun · agree to + verb · agree with a person
  • be proud to + verb · be proud of + noun / -ing · take pride in + noun / -ing
  • go shopping (the activity) · do the shopping (buy what you need)
  • along the river (next to it) · across the street (from one side to the other)
  • remember something yourself · remind somebody to do it
  • mission = an important task · job = paid work or a task · work = effort (uncountable)
